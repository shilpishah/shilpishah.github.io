Exploring Semantic Organization of Biomedical Concepts Using Clusters of Multi-word Expressions Sep 2025 – Dec 2025
Topics in AI: Natural Language Processing
  • Built a large-scale weighted MWE co-occurrence graph from MEDLINE abstracts (~32K nodes, 168K edges at t=10), applying frequency thresholding and recursive cluster splitting to induce interpretable semantic structure.
  • Designed and implemented a parameter sweep framework across co-occurrence thresholds, max/min cluster size constraints, and 5+ clustering algorithms (Leiden, Louvain, Chinese Whispers, HMM, agglomerative), systematically characterizing trade-offs in cluster granularity and semantic coherence.
